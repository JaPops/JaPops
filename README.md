# Hi, I'm Jaakko!

I'm studying game development at Taitotalo in Finland. I mainly work with Unity and C#, but I've also worked on level design, dialogue writing, lighting and UI.

During my studies, I've worked on four team projects. I enjoy building gameplay systems and figuring out how different parts of a game work together.

## Tools I use

- **Unity and C#** — gameplay programming and implementation
- **GitHub and GitHub Desktop** — version control and collaboration
- **Supabase** — online leaderboard and database
- **Trello, Miro and Discord** — planning and team communication
- **Excel** — documenting playtests and bugs

## Mystic Raccoon

A 3D sandbox game where a raccoon with magical powers explores a small town, moves objects and completes quests. Inspired by Goat Simulator and Untitled Goose Game.

<img width="480" height="320" alt="mystic-raccoon-gameplay" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dfb430ae-7f7d-48eb-bc5f-625b01aef4f9" />


**Team:** 6 people  
**Development time:** About 4 months  
**Status:** Playable Windows build, not publicly released

### My work

- Programmed the push, projectile and telekinesis spells.
- Implemented the spell selection UI.
- Built the pause and end screens.
- Implemented the score system and connected it to a Supabase online leaderboard.
- Worked on testing the spells, UI and scoring.

The spells needed to work with objects and quest systems made by other team members. The online leaderboard was also my first experience connecting a Unity game to a hosted database.

## Vape or Nope

A narrative 2D educational game made for A-klinikka. The player follows Luukas through his school day, talks to classmates and receives phone messages. Choices involve vaping, nicotine pouches and peer pressure.

**Team:** 5 people  
**Development time:** About 5–6 months  
**Status:** Browser test version available on itch.io

### My work

- Wrote the entire script, including conversations and player choices.
- Built the in-game phone system.
- Implemented message threads, unread notifications and dialogue events.
- Connected phone conversations to the player's progress and choices.

This project combined my writing and programming work: I could follow a conversation from the script to its implementation in the game.

## DreamJob

A third-person horror game with PS1-inspired graphics. You play as Alice, an unhappy office worker completing everyday tasks through pixel-art minigames. As the story progresses, both the office and its tasks become more unsettling.

**Team:** 7 people  
**Development time:** About 4–5 months  
**Status:** Completed playable build, not publicly released

### My work

- Implemented player movement, camera controls and shoulder switching.
- Built interaction mechanics, sprinting and stamina.
- Worked on the dialogue system and wrote dialogue.
- Implemented character-specific dialogue sounds.
- Created lighting and small cutscenes.
- Programmed menu functionality and the timer for the second phase.

This project taught me a lot about scope and priorities. We had more ideas than we could finish, and I revised the camera several times during development.

## Realms

A Geometry Dash-inspired 2D platformer where the player jumps over obstacles, collects points and tries to reach the finish line. The Realmshift mechanic lets the player switch to an upside-down world.

**Team:** 6 people  
**Development time:** About 3 months  
**Status:** Playable build, not publicly released

### My work

- Credited for level design and lead code.
- Programmed movement and jumping.
- Worked on audio functionality and menus.
- Helped with programming wherever the team needed support.
